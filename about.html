About Us

Research work that earns its result — not luck.

ZeroFluke Research Assistant is the research arm of ZeroFluke, built specifically for one job: getting academic work done properly.

Why "Zero Fluke"

A result that survives scrutiny isn't an accident. Every piece of work we take on is built to hold up — under a supervisor's questions, a defense panel, or a second look months later.

How we work

Requests are reviewed and handled directly, with the right person on the team assigned by field. There's no bidding, no browsing profiles — you tell us what you need, and we take it from there.

Who this is for

Undergraduate, Masters, and PhD researchers who need serious work done — not students looking for help with a routine assignment. We're selective about what we accept, on purpose.

Our approach

Revisions aren't an upsell — they're the process

Every order starts with a minimum of 10 revision rounds, because that's roughly how many times a supervisor actually reviews a piece of work before it's accepted. We built the pricing around that reality instead of pretending one draft is ever really "final."

For science-based research, fieldwork and apparatus costs are quoted separately and transparently — never buried in a flat fee that doesn't reflect what the work actually requires.
