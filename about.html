How It Started

The Story Behind ZeroFluke Research Assistant

During a Masters programme, we had friends who were having difficulty with their research, so we began to help them. We guided them through finding the right literature, working through difficult research problems, making sense of data, and generally helping them get their work done properly.

The referrals grew, and the requests started coming from people outside our immediate field of study, including students we had never met. It became clear that there was a real need for the kind of research support we were providing.

As the work grew, we understood that we couldn't cover every kind of research ourselves. Different subjects require different knowledge, methods, and experience. Rather than trying to become experts in everything, we began connecting with other researchers who knew their fields well and shared the same approach to doing research properly.

That was the beginning of what would become ZeroFluke Research Assistant.

Why It Matters

Research Shouldn't Compete with Survival

Research is one of the foundations of an academic certificate. Across much of Nigeria, students are stretched thin between coursework, research demands, and the realities of everyday life. Many are also trying to keep up with work, school fees, and other responsibilities while completing research that requires time, attention, and a proper understanding of the process. All these competing demands can make it difficult to find the time and focus needed to conduct rigorous research.

That is where ZeroFluke Research Assistant comes in. We work alongside students to reduce that burden, providing structured and dependable research support while helping them understand what is required at every stage. We assist with literature reviews, data analysis, findings, and other parts of the research process, helping students move their work forward properly and with the level of rigour academic research demands.

We are not here to take the work away from students. We are here to make the process more manageable, especially when time, knowledge, or experience becomes a barrier.

Every research request is matched with a researcher whose expertise aligns with the field and requirements of the work. This allows each project to be handled by someone who understands the subject, research methods, and academic standards relevant to it.

Our Work

We support students, academics, researchers, and organizations with research across different fields and at different stages of the research process. Much of this work is carried out behind the scenes and with a strong emphasis on confidentiality. We work according to the needs of each project, whether that means supporting an academic publication, assisting with student research, or carrying out research for an organization.

Revisions Aren't an Upsell. They're the Process

Built-In Revisions

Every research project comes with built-in revision rounds appropriate to the type and level of work. When a supervisor or reviewer provides feedback, you can bring those comments back and have them incorporated into the next version without paying a new fee for every correction. Feedback is part of research. We treat it that way.

Research Expenses

Some research projects require materials, fieldwork, laboratory reagents, specimens, or equipment that vary from one research project to another. These expenses are calculated separately and communicated before they are incurred. Where a client already has access to the required materials or can provide them, those resources can be used for the project. The same applies where fieldwork or laboratory work has already been conducted and the resulting data is available for us to analyse and interpret.
