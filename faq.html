Placing an order

Your academic level, field/programme, topic, referencing style, how many revisits you'd like, and your preferred deadline. Pricing updates live as you fill it in.
Each academic level has a base price and a standard number of included revisits. Extra revisits cost a flat fee each. Asking for the work sooner than our standard timeline adds a percentage of the base price per day earlier, but never below a minimum turnaround floor.

Revisits

It's when you upload your supervisor's comments on the work, and we revise it accordingly. Each academic level comes with a set number of revisits included.
You can top up more revisits at any point while your order is ongoing, at a flat fee per revisit.

Order status

Your dashboard lists every order you've placed with its current status: Request Pending (awaiting payment), Ongoing, Completed, or Cancelled.
