Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Effective date: August 30, 2026. Last updated: August 30, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how ZeroFluke Research Assistant ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our website and services. It is written to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to create your account, verify your identity, process your orders, and deliver our services.

Account information: Full name, email address, and password.

Identity verification: Your National Identification Number (NIN) and a photo of your NIN slip or card, submitted during signup. See Section 3 below for how this is specifically handled.

Order information: Academic level, field of study, research topic, institution, referencing style, deadline, and revisit preferences.

Payment information: Records of payments made (amount, payment type, transaction reference, status). We do not collect or store your card details directly; these are handled by our payment processor, Paystack.

Files you upload during an order: Supervisor comments and related documents you upload while your order is in progress, and the final delivered work.

Communications: Any messages, complaints, or support requests you send us through our contact form, including your name, email, and the content of your message.

Google Sign In information: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile information as provided by Google.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity and prevent duplicate or fraudulent accounts
  • Process and deliver your research orders
  • Process payments and refunds
  • Communicate with you about your orders, account, or support requests
  • Improve and maintain our services

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How We Handle Your NIN

Because your NIN is sensitive personal information, we want to be specific about exactly how it's used:

  • When you sign up, you upload a photo of your NIN slip or card and enter your NIN and name.
  • This photo is processed directly in your browser to check that the NIN number and name you entered match what's on the document.
  • We do not upload, store, or retain the photo of your NIN document on our servers or on any cloud storage. It is used only for that one time verification check and then discarded.
  • Your typed NIN number itself is used solely to confirm your identity at signup and to help prevent duplicate accounts or resolve disputes (for example, if someone falsely claims to have made a payment). It is not used for any other purpose, and is not shared with third parties beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

We work with a small number of trusted third parties to operate our service:

Paystack: processes all payments and refunds. Paystack receives your email and payment amount; we never receive or store your card details. See Paystack's Privacy Policy for details.

Google: provides the infrastructure behind our account system, file storage for order related documents, and optional Google Sign In. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Gmail: used to send you account verification, order updates, and support communications.

We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your account, order, and payment records are stored using Google's infrastructure (Google Sheets and Google Drive), protected by Google's account security and access controls. We restrict access to this information to what is necessary to operate the service.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted online.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • NIN document photo: Not retained at all (see Section 3).
  • Account and order information: Retained for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or maintain business records.
  • Payment records: Retained as required for financial and legal record keeping purposes.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to Section 7 below.

7. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal and record keeping obligations
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third party tracking cookies) to keep you signed in between visits. This stores basic session information such as your account ID and name locally on your device. This is essential to how the site functions and is not used for advertising or tracking across other websites.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for use by students and researchers and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

10. Data Breaches

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users.
